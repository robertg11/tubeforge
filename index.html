TubeForge Upload

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channels owned by its author.

What it does

TubeForge Upload is a small script that runs only on the owner's own computer. It publishes finished videos to YouTube channels that the owner personally manages. For each video it:

  • uploads the video file (videos.insert), as private or scheduled;
  • sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set);
  • adds caption files (captions.insert).

Every video is reviewed by the owner in YouTube Studio before it becomes public.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not access any channel that the owner does not manage.

Google and YouTube data

The tool uses the YouTube Data API with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl, only to publish the owner's own content. It does not collect, store or share data about other people. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact
